CONCEPT

Instrumentariumは、建築と音楽のあいだで、形や関係を移し替えるための道具群です。

線の長さを音の高さへ、座標を発音の時刻へ、空間に並ぶ線分を時間のなかで進む旋律へ。それぞれの道具は、異なる変換の規則と操作方法を備え、建築の形を音として聴き、音の関係から形を捉え直す機会をつくります。

変換の規則を変えれば、同じ形から異なる音楽が生まれます。演奏するように形に触れ、設計するように音の関係を組み立てる。その往復を通じて、見ること、聴くこと、つくることの接点を探ります。

建築を聴き、音楽に訪れるための道具の色々なあり方を考えます。

Instrumentarium is a collection of tools for transposing forms and relationships between architecture and music.

Line lengths become pitches, coordinates become the moments when notes begin, and line segments arranged in space become melodies unfolding in time. Each tool offers its own rules of transformation and ways of interacting, creating opportunities to hear architectural form as sound and to reconsider form through the relationships between sounds.

Change the rules of transformation, and the same form gives rise to different music. Touch form as though playing an instrument; compose relationships between sounds as though designing. Through this movement back and forth, we explore the points of contact between seeing, listening, and making.

We consider the many forms that tools might take for listening to architecture and visiting music.
